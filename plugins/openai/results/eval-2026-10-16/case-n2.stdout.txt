- **Militarism and rival alliances:** European powers built up their armies and formed opposing alliances, increasing tensions and making a wider war more likely.
- **Nationalism and imperial rivalry:** Competition for colonies and influence fueled distrust, while nationalist movements destabilized the Balkans and threatened Austria-Hungary.
- **Assassination and escalation:** The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand on June 28, 1914, triggered a crisis. Austria-Hungary’s confrontation with Serbia, alliance commitments, and military mobilizations turned it into a European war.
