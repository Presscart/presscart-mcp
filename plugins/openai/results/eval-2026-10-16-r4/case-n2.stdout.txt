- **Militarism and rival alliances:** European powers built up their armed forces and formed competing alliances, increasing tensions and making a wider war more likely.
- **Nationalism and imperial competition:** Rivalries over colonies, territory, and national identity fueled distrust, especially in the Balkans.
- **Assassination and diplomatic escalation:** The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand on June 28, 1914, triggered a crisis between Austria-Hungary and Serbia. Ultimatums, mobilizations, and declarations of war turned it into a European conflict.
