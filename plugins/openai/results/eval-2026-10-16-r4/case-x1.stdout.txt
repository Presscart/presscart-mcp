Your plan is worth exploring, with **buyer understanding as the primary goal**. Treat AI visibility and O-1 evidence as separate possibilities that need their own evaluation.

- **Buyer understanding:** Favor outlets your buyers trust, with relevant coverage and room to explain the product clearly. Distinguish independent reporting from sponsored content, and keep paid-placement disclosure explicit.
- **AI visibility:** Accessible, substantive coverage may give AI systems another source about your company. Publication alone does not guarantee indexing, citations, or inclusion in AI answers.
- **O-1 evidence:** Coverage may be relevant, but its value depends on the founder’s accomplishments, what the article says, the publication, and the overall petition. Paid coverage should not be assumed to carry the same weight as independent reporting. Have immigration counsel assess proposed placements before spending for this purpose.

Budget, dates, and outlets can remain open while we qualify the story. To start:

1. What does the product do, who buys it, and what do buyers currently misunderstand?
2. What news or evidence can support coverage—for example, customer results, original research, a launch, or a distinctive founder achievement?
3. How would you rank buyer education, AI visibility, and O-1 support? Is there an immigration deadline or counsel already involved?

Those answers will help determine whether to pursue earned coverage, disclosed sponsored explainers, or a mix—and what spending would be justified.
