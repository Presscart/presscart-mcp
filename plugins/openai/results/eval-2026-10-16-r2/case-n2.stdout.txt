- **Militarism and alliances:** European powers built up their armies and formed rival alliances, increasing tension and making a regional conflict more likely to spread.
- **Nationalism and imperial rivalry:** Competition for colonies and influence, alongside nationalist movements—especially in the Balkans—deepened hostility.
- **Assassination and escalation:** The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand in June 1914 triggered a crisis; ultimatums, mobilizations, and declarations of war turned it into a continent-wide conflict.
