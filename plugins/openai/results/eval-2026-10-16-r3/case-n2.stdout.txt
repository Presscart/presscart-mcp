- **Militarism and rival alliances:** European powers built up armies and navies, while alliance commitments increased the risk that a regional conflict would spread.
- **Nationalism and imperial rivalry:** Competition for colonies and influence heightened tensions, especially in the Balkans, where Serbian nationalism challenged Austria-Hungary.
- **Assassination and escalation:** The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand on June 28, 1914, triggered a crisis. Austria-Hungary’s confrontation with Serbia, German backing, and successive mobilizations and declarations of war turned it into a European war.
