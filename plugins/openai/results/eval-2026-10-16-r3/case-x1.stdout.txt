Your plan is worth exploring, with **buyer understanding as the primary goal**. AI visibility and O-1 evidence should be treated as possible secondary benefits, with separate standards for success.

| Goal | What would make coverage useful | Main limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer understanding | A relevant publication, clear product explanation, concrete use cases, and credible supporting evidence | A recognizable outlet name alone doesn’t establish audience fit or credibility |
| AI visibility | Publicly accessible, substantive coverage that identifies your company and product accurately | Placement cannot guarantee indexing, citations, or inclusion in AI answers |
| Founder’s O-1 evidence | Coverage that meaningfully discusses the founder and their work, with documented authorship, date, and publication context | Paid product promotion may have limited evidentiary value; immigration counsel should assess the proposed format |

Accepting paid-placement disclosure gives you more options. Keep the distinction between **sponsored content and independently earned editorial coverage** clear when evaluating offers and reporting results. Neither format guarantees buyer trust.

Before choosing outlets, dates, or budget, clarify:

- **Product and buyer:** What does the product do, who buys it, and what do buyers need to understand?
- **Story and proof:** What is newsworthy, and what can you substantiate through customer results, data, a demo, or founder expertise?
- **Priority:** Is success primarily buyer education, AI visibility, or evidence for a specific founder’s immigration case?
- **Distribution:** Which markets, languages, and industry audiences matter?

For any proposed placement, request the exact publication and content format, disclosure label, editorial process, expected publication window, URL permanence, and measurement approach. Avoid offers promising guaranteed AI citations or O-1 qualification.
